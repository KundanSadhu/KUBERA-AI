# Open-Source Inspiration Boundary

KUBERA’s workforce UX is informed by public ideas demonstrated by AI-workforce products such as Markus: plain-language requirements, explicit task graphs, named specialists, concurrent independent work, peer-review gates, a visible board, schedules/patrol, and durable auditability.

KUBERA is an independent implementation focused on local business records and validated Office deliverables. It does not import Markus source code, reuse its assets, copy its user interface, or inherit its broad product scope.

## KUBERA-specific differentiation

- Office artifacts (PPTX, DOCX, XLSX) are first-class outputs.
- Business records are the source of truth and provenance system.
- Analyst handoffs cite client/project record IDs.
- OfficeCLI is the only document-writing surface.
- The manager has a fixed depth-one deployment budget.
- No generic shell or coding tools are exposed to workers.
- Local SQLite, report files, schedules, and audit trails are designed for a desktop operator.

See [Markus-inspired workforce improvements](markus-inspired-workforce.md) for the precise adaptation.
